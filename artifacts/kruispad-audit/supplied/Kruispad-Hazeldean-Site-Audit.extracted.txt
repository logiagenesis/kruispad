5. Image system — this is the rebuild constraint
Client constraint, confirmed by the live CSS: they reuse the same photographs everywhere. Placement that cuts faces is a fail. The current theme does that on every inner page.

Homepage hero: full-bleed congregation photo with logo + title overlaid at the bottom. At 1920px the group is readable. The same file cannot be reused as a 215px-tall strip without destroying faces.

5.3 Hard image rules for the rebuild (do not break these)
Never put a group photo into a short header strip. If the congregation photo stays, it is a hero only, min-height large enough that front-row faces are fully visible, or it is not used.
Staff photos: one aspect ratio only. Recrop KPSTAFF originals to a consistent 4:5 or 1:1 with the face in the upper third. Do not reuse Elementor auto-thumbs.
KPmoments portraits stay portraits, or get a manual landscape crop signed off. Auto object-fit:cover on a 16:9 box will cut faces — that is the current fail.
Keep their existing photos. Do not replace with stock. Improve framing, colour, and compression.
Every image gets alt text. Current score: 2 of 280.
Bank / SnapScan graphic must remain readable at mobile width, or the numbers must move into HTML (text) so screen readers and search can see them.
Do not serve 2560px portraits as CSS backgrounds without srcset. Current Ken Burns slides pull the -scaled 1707px file into a 765px box.

8. Bigger picture — what a rebuild must not break
Fixing the header crop by deleting the congregation photo will be treated as a content failure. The photo stays; the container changes.
The church runs two physical places. The rebuild must keep Office vs Erediens distinct on every contact surface. Merging them into one pin is a functional fail.
Sunday rhythm that must remain findable in one glance: 09:30 erediens, Suzi/Odos during the morning service, 17:30 Café Berea. That rhythm is currently split across three pages and missing from the homepage.
Eleos and Kairos are separate NPOs with their own domains. The church site should introduce them and hand off. Do not absorb their sites.
Odos is a third-party curriculum (odos.co.za). Keep the Sunday-kids explanation on this domain; do not turn the church site into the Odos catalogue.
Giving: the SnapScan QR and Standard Bank record are the operational source. If they become HTML text, the QR still has to work on mobile. Do not invent new account details.
Afrikaans is the site language. A few Eleos/Kairos blocks are English. Rebuild should pick a language rule and stick to it; do not silently translate the creed.
URL aliases already exist (/preke-en-media/cafe-berea/ → /cafe-berea/, /home/odos-kidz/ → /odos-kidz/, /meer-oor-ons/organisasies/ → /groepe/organisasies/). New site must 301 the current canonicals plus these aliases or inbound links die.
GitHub repo is empty. It is the destination for the rebuild, not a source. Do not assume a stack from the repo.
9. Rebuild sequence that does not strand content
Freeze the asset set: originals stored under artifacts/kruispad-audit/assets/ named {id}_{filename}. Do not start layout until staff + hero crops are chosen from these files.
Write the IA as seven user jobs: Visit Sunday, Meet the people, Kids, Young adults / seniors, Give, Office, Partners (Eleos/Kairos/Odos). Map each current URL into one job. Kill empty pages (Kalender-as-placeholder, Preke-without-player) or fill them before launch.
Build the image components first: HeroFull, HeroSafe (face-aware), StaffCard (fixed 4:5, object-position per person), MomentPortrait, Logo. No generic ImageBox.
Put times, both addresses, phone, and the four working emails in a single source of truth. Every page footer reads from that. Delete the three misspelled Christiaan/admin variants.
Redirect matrix before DNS cutover: all 17 canonicals + the aliases listed in section 3 and 8 + /droom-vir-kruispad/ + /?page_id=485.
Only then design visual 10x. Visual work on top of a broken IA re-creates this site with nicer CSS.
